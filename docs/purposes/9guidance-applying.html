9 Develop guidance for understanding and applying methods

Alternative labels: methods guidance; tutorial; best practice recommendations

Definition

These studies aim to develop guidance that helps health researchers or other interest-holders understand, select, and apply methods. Guidance may include conceptual explanations, examples, recommendations for or against specific methods, or tools that operationalize the guidance, such as decision algorithms, step-by-step tutorials, templates, or code for statistical software.

Typical approaches and corresponding examples

Approaches to develop methods guidance greatly vary and include: Theory-based methods research: Covariate adjustment in trials;1 credibility criteria for subgroup effects, some criteria based on theoretical reasoning.2

Simulation study: Guidance on using matching in cluster trials;3 Guidance for variable screening in high dimensional survival analysis;4 estimation of diagnostic test accuracy without reference standard.5

Experimental analysis of real datasets: Guidance for using machine learning for abstract screening;6 Framework for prospective meta-analysis, tested in 4 real projects.7

Qualitative study involving researchers or other interest-holders in health research: Q-methodology, focus groups, content analysis, and workshops to produce guidance on creating summaries for trial participants.8

Consensus study among researchers or other interest-holders in health research: Guidance on factors to consider when inviting multiple interest-holder groups to research, based on Delphi;9 Guidance on handling missing data in trials, based on Nominal Group Technique.10

Review of applied methods: recommendations for the use of directed acyclic graphs.11

Scoping review of methods research: A scoping review of methods papers informed guidance on integrating qualitative methods in randomized trials of complex interventions;12 A scoping review of methods papers informed guidance for developing core outcome sets.13

Qualitative synthesis of methods research: Review with qualitative synthesis of guidance for the evaluation and updating of clinical prediction models;14 review of methods research on saturation in qualitative studies;15 review of methods for confidence intervals in adaptive trials.16

Informal approaches: preventing common misunderstandings of randomisation;17 tutorial for sample size calculation in cluster trials;18 explanation why guidelines should seldom make good practice statements.19
