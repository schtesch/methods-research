7 Evaluate a single method

Definition

These studies aim to assess a single method with respect to precision, bias, acceptance, variability introduced by methods users, or other outcomes. This type includes proof-of-concept studies that demonstrate whether a method can work in principle before larger-scale testing.

TipBoundaries with related objectives

14 Assess variability of results across methodological choices: In O7, researchers evaluate one specific method, and the variability that users introduce when applying it can be one outcome of that evaluation. When researchers do not evaluate a specific method and instead study how much results vary across methodological choices, the objective becomes O14.

Typical approaches and corresponding examples

Simulation studies: random-effects meta-analysis;1 false positives when conducting subgroup analysis.2

Reliability and agreement studies: reliability when 6 raters applied a risk of bias tool to 60 observational studies;3 reliability when 12 raters applied a quality assessment tool for radiomics to 35 studies at two time points;4 agreement between nine research teams attempting to reproduce the same observational study;5 agreement between reproduced and original analyses of 150 real-world evidence studies.6

Qualitative studies: focus group addressing the clarity of plain language recommendations in guidelines;7 interviews with users of a new instrument addressing face validity.8

Reviews of applied or planned methods: concordance of target trial emulations with benchmark randomized controlled trials.9
