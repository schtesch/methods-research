12 Qualitative syntheses of methods research

Terminology: qualitative systematic reviews of methods research

Definition and features

In qualitative syntheses of methods research, researchers synthesize findings, concepts, explanations, recommendations, or arguments from text-rich methods research, such as interview studies or guidance documents. They usually start with a focused qualitative question, search for eligible publications using a comprehensive or purposeful sampling strategy, assess study quality, and use an explicit qualitative synthesis method, such as thematic synthesis.

  • A strength is that they can integrate concepts or explanations across studies.
  • Key challenges are the difficult search and the lack of quality criteria for qualitative methods research.
TipBoundaries with related types

Boundary with A11 scoping review. Both use systematic searches and may include qualitative categorization but scoping reviews do not typically include formal quality assessment or an interpretive synthesis of findings. The distinction can be difficult in practice, especially when a scoping review goes beyond description or when a qualitative synthesis mainly maps the literature.

TipA remaining disagreement: do scoping reviews and qualitative syntheses differ enough?

Our group did not resolve whether scoping reviews of methods research differ sufficiently from qualitative syntheses of methods research, since we struggled to differentiate them in practice.

Viewing the types as archetypes rather than direct reflections of current practice helped us decide to keep them separate, so that the typology can support clearer design and reporting of future studies.

Typical objectives and corresponding examples

Summarize the results of methods research: meta-ethnographic synthesis of qualitative studies assessing the reasons for non-retention in clinical trials;1 synthesis of studies assessing factors that impact on trial recruitment.2

Develop guidance for understanding and applying methods: review with qualitative synthesis of guidance for the evaluation and updating of clinical prediction models;3 review of methods research on saturation in qualitative studies.4
