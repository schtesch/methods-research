#### Distinction between literature-based types of methods research.

| Characteristic | Review of applied methods (A9) | Meta-epidemiological study (A10) | Scoping review of MR (A11) | Qualitative synthesis of MR (A12) | Quantitative synthesis of MR (A13) |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Typical purpose | Assess methodological practice or quality | Assess impact of methodological characteristics on study results | Identify available methods or MR on a specific topic | Synthesize findings of MR qualitatively | Synthesize findings of MR quantitatively |
| Included publications (unit of analysis) | Individual applied studies  | Individual applied studies  | Methods research papers | Methods research papers | Methods research papers |
| Sampling strategy | Random, purposeful, stratified, matched, or completea  | Stratified, matched, or completea | Completea   | Completea or  purposeful | Completea |
| Typical analytic approach | Qualitative categorisation and descriptive statistics | Meta-regression | Qualitative categorisation and descriptive statistics | Qualitative synthesis | Meta-analysis |
| Role of quality assessment  | Often as an outcome  | Possible as an exposure or covariable | Usually not performed | Standard step in synthesis processb | Standard step in synthesis processb |
| Analogy in health research | Observational study (prevalence) | Observational study (exposure-outcome) | Scoping review[(Munn2022; Nussbaumer-Streit2025)](https://paperpile.com/c/n89WZN/FPM7a+wjzT8)  | Qualitative evidence synthesis[(*Cochrane-Campbell Handbook for Qualitative Evidence Synthesis*, n.d.; Nussbaumer-Streit2025)](https://paperpile.com/c/n89WZN/wjzT8+qz0SN) | Quantitative evidence synthesis[(Higgins JPT, Thomas J, Chandler J, Cumpston M, Li T, Page MJ, et al, n.d.; Nussbaumer-Streit2025)](https://paperpile.com/c/n89WZN/wjzT8+r6Gvr) |

#### a Usually based on a comprehensive search process that aims to find all eligible publications as completely as possible.

b Quality criteria currently remain unclear for most types of MR.
